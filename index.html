Brief Bio

Prof. Souradyuti Paul is Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai. His research interests include cryptography, cybersecurity, and financial technology. He leads the institute’s FinTech Security project under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology’s ISEA-III programme. Earlier, he served as an Associate Professor at IIT Bhilai and as an Assistant Professor at IIT Gandhinagar. He holds a BE from Jadavpur University, an MTech from the Indian Statistical Institute, and a PhD from KU Leuven, Belgium.

Before joining academia, he spent about one and a half decades as a doctoral, postdoctoral, and guest researcher at leading data security groups across Europe, the USA, and Canada, with long-term engagements at KU Leuven, NIST (USA), and the University of Waterloo (Canada).

Beyond his research, he has played a significant role in global cryptographic standardisation. He was a major technical contributor to ECRYPT-eSTREAM, the European Union’s stream cipher project (2005–2008), and served on the evaluation committee for SHA-3, the US Government’s cryptographic hash function standard organised by the Department of Commerce. In India, he has been nominated to lead multiple Bureau of Indian Standards initiatives on blockchain-based applications under various ISO projects.

He is also actively engaged in developmental work in Chhattisgarh. As co-founder and founding Convener of the IIT Bhilai Cell on Economic Development of Chhattisgarh (IC-EDC), he works with government and development partners on rural water security and community development. He is the Chief Coordinator of the Dantewada District Development Design Project, funded by the district administration, and a Coordinator of the Vidya Samiksha Kendra (VSK) Project, funded by the state government.
