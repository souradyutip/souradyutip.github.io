About

Souradyuti Paul is currently Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bhilai.

Earlier, he served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of CSE at IIT Gandhinagar (July 2014 – October 2017) and as an Associate Professor at IIT Bhilai (October 2017 – February 2026). He holds a BE from Jadavpur University, an MTech from the Indian Statistical Institute, and a PhD from KU Leuven, Belgium.

In India, he has led several initiatives on the standardization of blockchain-based applications through BIS within the ISO framework.

Prof. Paul is actively engaged in developmental initiatives in Chhattisgarh. He serves as the Chief Coordinator of the Dantewada District Development Design project, as a Coordinator of the Vidya Samiksha Kendra (VSK) project, and as the convener of the IIT Bhilai Cell on Economic Development of Chhattisgarh.
